## M5 metric contract and planned extensions

- **Recorded units:** sum of observed nonnegative integer units. This measures realized recorded sales, not latent demand.
- **Estimated sales value (USD proxy):** when units=0, value=0.00 whether or not a price is present; when units>0, multiply by the matching published weekly average sell_price. Positive sales without a price abort this fact build. Preserve the absent price as NULL and has_price=false; never substitute a zero price. The zero-quantity convention describes recorded sales value only, not lost sales or demand. Expose daily-row, positive-row and unit-weighted coverage separately. Use DECIMAL(20,0) for the units operand so DuckDB evaluates the product as DECIMAL(38,2), then store DECIMAL(28,2); no floating-point multiplication or rounding of source cents. Do not call it verified transaction revenue, net revenue, margin or customer spend.
- **Category/department/product share:** units or estimated value divided by the all-category total under the same date/store scope. SQL18 fixes all 10 stores and 2016-01-26–2016-04-24 inclusive (90 days), before the final forecast holdout. Aggregate exact amounts before dividing; display percentages to four decimals. Zero denominator produces NULL, not zero or 100%. Rounded category shares may sum to 99.9999 or 100.0001. The SKU count includes all distinct source item IDs in the window, even with zero units; it is not an active-availability count. This window/cutoff also anchors the accepted 90-day ABC baseline, but category contribution itself is not ABC classification. SQL19 adds seven source departments under the same window. Its global unit/value shares use all categories; within_category_value_share_pct divides each department value by its own category value. TOTAL has no parent category and its within-category share is NULL. Category denominators are calculated from category aggregates; department rollups must reproduce their unrounded counts/amounts. Anonymous source department codes are retained without invented subcategory descriptions.
- **Product-unit concentration:** SQL20 uses the same 90-day window and sums each item across all 10 stores. Rank strictly positive item totals by units descending then item_id ascending, with explicit ROWS cumulative sums. TOP_10/50/100 take up to that many positive SKUs; REACH_80/95 take the smallest prefix whose exact integer cumulative units meet or cross the target. Include the crossing SKU. Equal-unit ties are deterministic but may straddle a threshold; they do not imply different business value. SKU shares use all source items including zeros; unit shares use all recorded units. ALL_POSITIVE, ZERO_SALES and ALL_PRODUCTS provide controls; the eight report rows overlap. For zero overall units, reach counts/units/shares are NULL and status N/A. No monetary values influence ranking, no 80/20 pattern is assumed, and no ABC labels are assigned by this query.
- **Units-based ABC baseline:** fixed 2016-01-26–2016-04-24, pool all stores and categories into one SKU ranking. Sort strictly positive units descending then item_id ascending. ZERO_SALES is assigned first for zero units; its rank/cumulative units are NULL. For positives, prior cumulative units = cumulative units minus current units: if prior×100 < total×80 assign A; otherwise if prior×100 < total×95 assign B; otherwise C. This includes the crossing SKU in the earlier band. At exactly 80% before a SKU, that next SKU begins B; exactly 95% begins C. A equals the smallest 80% prefix, A+B the smallest 95% prefix; a dominant SKU can make B empty. Compare integers, not rounded shares. ABC labels are global volume-contribution groups, not per-store/per-category, value/profit or volatility classes. The 80/95 thresholds are project choices; baseline execution and subsequent sensitivity review are separate steps.

## Planned Retailrocket metrics

- **Visitors:** distinct anonymous visitorid within the stated eligible population/window; not verified people.
- **Behavioural RF:** recency and frequency of separately defined views, carts and recorded transactions. No monetary component without an interpretable monetary field.
- **Session:** proposed 30 minutes of inactivity boundary per visitor; a derived convention, not a supplied session ID. Validate ties and compare alternative thresholds.
- **Cart conversion proxy:** fraction of eligible deduplicated cart episodes with a later observed transaction for the same visitor/item within 24 hours. Same-time ambiguity is reported. Episodes in the final 24 hours are censored; lack of purchase does not prove abandonment. Audit repeated carts and pre-existing purchase events before freezing the denominator.
- **Co-purchase rules:** unique baskets containing both items / all eligible baskets for support; both / antecedent baskets for confidence; confidence / consequent prevalence for lift. Require minimum co-occurrence and assess later-period stability. Quantity and basket value are unavailable unless explicitly documented.
- **Availability coverage:** proportion with a known prior `available` value; separate known unavailable from unknown. Source observations are not a live inventory ledger.
